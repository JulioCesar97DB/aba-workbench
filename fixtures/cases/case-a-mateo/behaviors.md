# Target behaviors: definitions, baselines and hypothesized functions

Prepared by Daniela Muestra, M.S., BCBA. Baselines come from the three direct observations (10/06/2026 home, 2 hours; 10/09/2026 park and grocery store, 1.5 hours; 10/13/2026 home morning routine, 2 hours). Invented case.

## 1. Elopement

- Definition: Mateo moves more than 3 feet away from the supervising adult, or leaves the designated area (house, fenced yard, store aisle, park play area), without permission, by walking fast or running. Includes opening an outside door and leaving the house. Non-examples: walking to the bathroom or his bedroom inside the house; running inside the fenced play area.
- Baseline: 2, 4 and 1 episodes across the three observations (7 episodes in 5.5 hours, about 1.3 per hour). Mother's estimate: 4 to 6 per day.
- Intensity: high. Two September incidents in traffic (see parent interview); on 10/09 he ran about 40 feet toward a parking lot.
- Hypothesized function: escape from transitions and demands (primary); access to preferred items and places (secondary). FAST escape 4 of 4; MAS escape 4.8.

## 2. Physical aggression

- Definition: hitting with an open hand or fist, biting, scratching or kicking another person with contact. Non-examples: accidental contact during play; tapping an adult's arm to get attention.
- Baseline: 9, 5 and 11 episodes (25 episodes in 5.5 hours, about 4.5 per hour). 1 bite observed (10/13).
- Intensity: moderate to high. The 10/13 bite left teeth marks without breaking the skin; the mother has bruises on both forearms; his sister was bitten on the shoulder in August.
- Hypothesized function: access to preferred items, mainly the tablet (primary); escape from self-care demands (secondary). FAST attention/preferred items 4 of 4, escape 3 of 4.

## 3. Tantrums

- Definition: an episode with at least two of crying with vocalization, screaming above conversational volume, dropping to the floor, or kicking, lasting 30 seconds or more; the episode ends after 60 seconds without these behaviors. Non-example: whining for less than 30 seconds.
- Baseline: 4, 3 and 5 episodes (12 episodes in 5.5 hours). Durations from 3 to 21 minutes; mean 9.8 minutes.
- Hypothesized function: escape from transitions and demands, and access to preferred items. FAST 4 of 4 for both.

## 4. Head hitting (self-injury)

- Definition: striking his own forehead or head with an open palm or fist, or hitting his head against a wall, floor or furniture, with audible contact. Non-example: touching or rubbing his head.
- Baseline: 3, 1 and 4 episodes (8 episodes in 5.5 hours). 6 of 8 occurred during tantrums; 2 occurred while he was alone waiting for a video to load.
- Intensity: up to about 10 hits in 20 seconds; redness for about 20 minutes; no tissue damage observed. Mother reports a bump on the forehead after hitting the floor in July (no medical visit).
- Hypothesized function: escape during tantrums; automatic (sensory) when alone. FAST sensory 3 of 4; MAS sensory 4.0, escape 3.3.

## 5. Throwing objects

- Definition: throwing an object more than 2 feet outside a play activity. Non-example: throwing a ball during a ball game.
- Baseline: 6, 0 and 7 episodes (13 in 5.5 hours); items included plates with food (3), toy cars and the tablet.
- Hypothesized function: escape from meal demands and from non-preferred foods (primary); access to preferred items (secondary). MAS escape 4.3, tangible 3.8.

## Skill deficits noted for replacement and acquisition goals

Functional communication (requesting items, help and a break with a word or picture), accepting "wait" and "no", following one-step instructions, staying with an adult and holding hands in the community, toileting, accepting new foods on the plate, dressing, hand washing, responding to his name, joint attention, imitation and play with his sister.
