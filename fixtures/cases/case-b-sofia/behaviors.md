# Target behaviors: definitions, baselines and hypothesized functions

Prepared by Daniela Muestra, M.S., BCBA. Baselines come from the three direct observations (10/07/2026 home, 2 hours; 10/10/2026 library and park, 2 hours; 10/14/2026 home evening routine, 2 hours). School data come from the IEP and the mother. Invented case.

## 1. Physical aggression

- Definition: hitting, kicking, pushing or shoving another person with contact. Non-examples: accidental contact in play; high fives.
- Baseline: 3, 1 and 2 episodes (6 episodes in 6 hours). School: about 2 per week (IEP). Home, mother's estimate: several per day, mostly toward Diego.
- Intensity: moderate. Bruise on Diego's upper arm (mother's report); 1-day suspension in May 2026 after kicking a classmate.
- Hypothesized function: escape from demands (primary); access to preferred items (secondary). FAST escape 4 of 4; MAS escape 4.5, tangible 3.8.

## 2. Verbal outbursts

- Definition: yelling, screaming, insults or threats above conversational volume, for 30 seconds or more; the episode ends after 2 minutes of calm. Non-example: raising her voice briefly when excited about a topic.
- Baseline: 2, 1 and 3 episodes (6 episodes in 6 hours); durations 6 to 15 minutes, mean 9.7 minutes. School: almost daily (IEP).
- Hypothesized function: escape from demands and transitions. FAST escape 4 of 4.

## 3. Property destruction

- Definition: tearing, breaking, throwing or kicking objects so that they are damaged or could be. Non-example: crumpling scrap paper to throw it away.
- Baseline: 2, 0 and 1 episodes (3 in 6 hours). Mother: tears homework about half the times it is assigned; broke the TV remote on 10/01; tore a library book in September. IEP: threw a chair on 03/12/2026.
- Hypothesized function: escape from demands. FAST escape 4 of 4.

## 4. Leaving the area and school refusal

- Definition: leaving the assigned area without permission (classroom, group, adult's side in the community), or refusing to leave for school (crying, locking herself in, refusing to board the bus) so that she misses or arrives late.
- Baseline: 1 episode in community observation (ran across a street). IEP: leaves the classroom 2 to 4 times per week. Mother: refused school on 6 of 20 school days in October.
- Intensity: high for street crossings; school refusal affects attendance and the mother's work.
- Hypothesized function: escape from academic and social demands. MAS escape 5.0.

## 5. Hand biting (self-injury)

- Definition: biting her own hand or wrist with enough force to leave a mark. Non-example: nail biting.
- Baseline: 2, 0 and 1 episodes (3 in 6 hours); red marks, no broken skin. Mother reports a head-banging episode on 09/14/2026 that needed urgent care (cut closed with skin glue; no record on file).
- Hypothesized function: escape (primary); automatic (secondary). FAST escape 3 of 4, sensory 2 of 4.

## Skill deficits noted for replacement and acquisition goals

Requesting a break or help appropriately, accepting changes in routine, coping and calming strategies, conversational turn-taking and reading peers, joining peer play, completing homework with breaks, safe street crossing, showering and hair washing independently, ordering and paying in a store.
