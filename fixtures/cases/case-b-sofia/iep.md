# Individual Education Plan (IEP): excerpts

Ejemplo Elementary School (invented). Annual IEP meeting held 04/17/2026; plan in effect 04/17/2026 to 04/16/2027. Excerpts copied by the BCBA from the copy the mother provided. Invented case.

**Eligibility.** Primary exceptionality: Autism Spectrum Disorder. Secondary: Other Health Impairment (ADHD).

**Present levels of performance (from the IEP).**
- Reading: on grade level (4th grade); strong decoding and vocabulary.
- Math: about one year below grade level; does not finish independent work.
- Communication: uses complete sentences; difficulty with turn-taking in conversation, staying on topic and understanding nonliteral language.
- Social/emotional/behavioral: "Sofía leaves the classroom without permission 2 to 4 times per week, usually during writing tasks or group work, and is found in the hallway or the media center. She has physical altercations with peers (pushing, kicking) about 2 times per week, mostly at lunch and recess. Verbal outbursts (yelling, insults) occur almost daily when tasks are changed or when she loses a game."
- Teacher comment recorded in the IEP: "On 03/12/2026 Sofía threw a chair when asked to move from the computer to her writing assignment; the class was evacuated to the hallway while she calmed down (about 25 minutes)."

**Services.**
- Specialized instruction through support facilitation in general education: 300 minutes per week.
- Speech-language therapy (pragmatic language): 60 minutes per week.
- School counseling: 30 minutes per week.

**Accommodations.** Visual schedule; break card (up to 3 breaks of 5 minutes per day); preferential seating away from the door; extended time on assignments; advance warning before transitions; access to headphones in the cafeteria.

**Annual goals (selected).**
- Given a visual cue, Sofía will use her break card instead of leaving the classroom in 4 of 5 opportunities.
- During structured peer activities, Sofía will take at least 3 conversational turns on a topic chosen by a peer in 4 of 5 opportunities.
- When frustrated, Sofía will use a taught calming strategy within 2 minutes in 3 of 5 observed opportunities.

**Behavior plan.** "A school-based Functional Behavior Assessment was requested on 04/17/2026 and is pending."

**Behavior analysis.** The IEP does not include behavior analysis services.
