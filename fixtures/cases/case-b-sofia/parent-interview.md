# Parent interview: BCBA's notes

Notes by Daniela Muestra, M.S., BCBA. Interview at home with Carmen Inventada (mother) on 10/05/2026, 90 minutes, in English. Invented case.

**Family.** Sofía lives with her mother, her brother Diego (6) and her maternal grandmother (72), who looks after the children from 3:00 to 5:30 p.m. while the mother works (8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.). The father lives in another state and has no regular contact.

**History.** Born at 39 weeks, no complications. Early milestones on time; talked in sentences by age 2. Teachers raised concerns in kindergarten about outbursts and playing alone. Six months of school counseling in 2024, which the mother says "did not change anything". Never received ABA.

**Medication.** Methylphenidate ER 18 mg every morning since March 2026; the mother says she focuses better in the morning, but outbursts in the afternoon are "worse than before".

**Behaviors at home.**
- Hits, kicks and pushes Diego "every day, several times", mostly over the tablet and the TV remote. Diego had a bruise on his upper arm last week.
- Explosive outbursts: yells insults, screams, and has said "I'll kill you" to her brother and "I hate you, I'll run away" to her mother. Outbursts last "10 minutes, sometimes half an hour", nearly every day after school and at bedtime.
- Breaks and tears things when angry: ripped her homework "about half the time" she is asked to do it, broke the TV remote on 10/01, and tore a school library book in September (the mother paid for it).
- Bites her own hand and wrist when frustrated; sometimes leaves marks.
- On 09/14/2026 she banged her head against the bedroom wall during an outburst and got a small cut above her eyebrow; the grandmother took her to urgent care, where it was cleaned and closed with skin glue. The mother does not have the urgent care paperwork.
- Needs the same evening routine every day; if dinner is late or the route home changes, she screams and refuses to get out of the car.

**School.**
- Fourth grade at Ejemplo Elementary School, general education classroom with ESE support. Has an IEP.
- Refused to go to school on 6 of 20 school days in October: cried, locked herself in the bathroom, or would not get on the bus. On those days the mother arrived late to work or the grandmother kept her home.
- Suspended for 1 day in May 2026 after kicking a classmate at recess.
- The teacher sends notes home about her leaving the classroom and arguing with peers.

**Daily living.**
- Showers only with repeated reminders and arguments; a shower can take 45 minutes of prompting; she will not wash her hair on her own.
- Can dress herself but chooses the same two outfits; melts down when they are in the wash.
- Does not cross streets safely: runs across without looking when excited; the mother holds her wrist in parking lots.
- Cannot order food or pay at a store; becomes upset if a cashier talks to her.

**Social.** No friends at school or in the neighborhood; was not invited to any birthday party this year. Talks about trains for long stretches. Plays alone on the tablet.

**Strengths.** Reads well and loves books about trains; good memory; affectionate with her grandmother; helps feed the family dog; likes drawing maps.

**Mother's goals.** "That she stops hurting her brother." "That she can calm down without breaking things." "That she goes to school without a fight every morning." "That she has at least one friend." "That she showers on her own."

**Mother's situation.** Says she feels "exhausted and judged" and has started avoiding outings. Can join parent training on weekday evenings after 5:30 p.m. and on Saturday mornings. The grandmother is willing to learn the strategies but speaks only Spanish.

**Availability.** School 8:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Speech at school (part of the IEP). Psychiatry follow-up every 6 weeks. Available for sessions after 3:30 p.m. on weekdays and Saturday mornings.
