# Reading the standardized score reports

How to read the two instruments the payers require. Report numbers exactly as the score report prints them; this file only explains what they mean.

## Vineland-3

- The Adaptive Behavior Composite and the domain standard scores (Communication, Daily Living Skills, Socialization, and Motor Skills for younger children) have a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15. Subdomain v-scale scores have a mean of 15 and a standard deviation of 3.
- Lower adaptive scores mean more impairment.
- On the Maladaptive Behavior domain the direction reverses: higher v-scale scores (Internalizing, Externalizing, Maladaptive Behavior Index) mean more problem behavior.

## BASC-3 Parenting Relationship Questionnaire (PRQ)

- T-scores have a mean of 50 and a standard deviation of 10.
- Scales: Attachment, Communication, Discipline Practices, Involvement, Parenting Confidence and Relational Frustration; the Child/Adolescent form (ages 6 to 18) adds Satisfaction with School, which the Preschool form does not have.
- Low scores on Attachment, Communication, Discipline Practices, Involvement, Parenting Confidence and Satisfaction with School are the areas of concern; on Relational Frustration, high scores are.

## Levels

Use the adaptive levels and classifications printed on each report (Low, Moderately Low, Elevated, At-Risk, Clinically Significant and so on). Do not assign a level the report does not give.
