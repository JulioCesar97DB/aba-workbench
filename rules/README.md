# Writing rules

These are the instructions the model receives, kept apart from the code so they can be read, discussed and versioned.

| File | What it holds | When it is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assessment.md` | Rules for the whole assessment: what it is for, who reads it, how it is written, "nothing invented", the four kinds of content (facts; clinical reasoning, where "ABA will…" states only what the child needs to learn and the general approach; her standard practice, as these rules state it from her confirmed answers or as her standing instructions give it; proposals, only in the case formulation, never in prose until she accepts them), the format for pasting into ABA Matrix. | On every call, as part of the cached prefix. |
| `guide.md` | The assessment as a whole: what it is for, the clinical reasoning chain (sources, deficits and behaviors, functions, interventions, goals, caregiver training, generalization, discharge, recommendations), the map of all 22 sections (what each owns, refers to and must match) and what must match everywhere. | On every call, whole, as part of the cached prefix (owner's decision, 2026-11-23). |
| `style.md` | How she writes: her reader, voice and person (professionals by role, caregivers by relation, never "the BCBA observed"), attribution without dates, no quotes, abbreviations, conflicting sources, her patterns ("is maintained by"), explaining numbers, the words and sentences she would not write, terms (no severity level), length. Built from her questionnaire answers of 2026-11-27 and her patterns. | On every call, whole, as part of the cached prefix (owner's decision, 2026-11-23). |
| `examples.md` | Invented examples in her style, about an invented child: a Medical Necessity deficit paragraph that explains rather than lists (with a weak version for contrast) and a whole Strengths and Weaknesses section. Never real client text, and no facts shared with the invented evaluation cases in `fixtures/cases/`: a new evaluation case must not reuse the examples' child, people, behaviors, settings or interests either (`tests/rules.test.ts` checks the names). | On every call, whole, as part of the cached prefix (owner's decision, 2026-11-23). |
| `payers/florida-medicaid-molina.md` | Facts from the Florida Medicaid policy (Dec. 2024) and the Molina guide (Jul. 2026) that bear on authorization: who qualifies, the instruments, why requests are returned, intensity, what the assessment and plan must contain (6.2.2), the FBA and behavior plan, schedule, transition and discharge; then our mapping of them onto the sections and a payer readiness list. Only what those two texts say, except the mapping, which is marked as ours. | On every call, as part of the cached prefix. |
| `instruments.md` | How to read the Vineland-3 and the BASC-3 PRQ (means, standard deviations, the direction of each scale), the FBA tools (FAST, MAS, ABC and scatterplot data), the ABLLS-R, preference assessments, and how indirect and direct evidence combine into a hypothesized function. Not from the payers; where each result goes and how a function is written are confirmed by her questionnaire answers of 2026-11-27. | On every call, as part of the cached prefix. |
| `interventions.md` | What she uses and never uses in a plan (no extinction or punishment, the procedures she avoids, the ones she adds, DRO and automatic reinforcement), how Hypothesis Based Interventions is laid out and how a glossary entry is written. From her questionnaire answers of 2026-11-27. | Not sent yet: the Plan step (sections 14 and 15) will load it, with `programs.md`. It is not part of the cached prefix. The points that bear on sections drafted today are also in those sections' rules. |
| `programs.md` | Replacements (one functionally equivalent per function), how many programs (about 25 under 5; about one per weekly hour, 30, from 5), which skills and where they come from, how a program is written (phrases in English, a task analysis of 4 to 6 steps with no physiological step), baselines from 10 opportunities, mastery criteria (95% for safety, 100% for crossing the street), and the caregiver goals (one set, a fourth for safety or home, baselines of 0% or 10 to 20%). Her defaults from her questionnaire answers of 2026-11-27, in the rules for every user (owner's decision, 2026-11-28). | Not sent yet: the Plan step will load it with `interventions.md`. It is not part of the cached prefix. |
| `requests.md` | How each request of the section workspace works: her sections (final, never contradicted), her standing instructions, her session notes (used in the section that owns their fact), and the kinds of request (draft, change, paragraph change, improve; a shorter text keeps its incidents). | On every request of the section workspace, as part of the cached prefix. Not in `pnpm phase0`. |
| `case-map.md` | Drafting the case map: whose word comes first (her sections, her map, her notes, the sources), claims with verbatim quotes, the four kinds of content in the map (her standard practice from her standing instructions or her confirmed defaults), each part, intensity left to her (the app suggests it from her rule), ids, her notes as facts, and the report on her sections. | Only in the task of the case map's two requests (D15), never in a section's. |
| `behaviors.md` | The target behaviors: a definition from what this child does (onset, offset, counting, exclusions, examples and non-examples), her default thresholds (from her questionnaire answers; her sections, then the sources for this child, then her standing instructions override them, one threshold at a time), 8 to 14 behaviors with generic names, examples that cover the functions, precursors inside the definition, frequency by default, the caregiver's count for a behavior not observed, baselines, the function as a hypothesis with its evidence, her merge rule for groups, barriers and risks, and one invented definition example (kept out of `examples.md`, so the prefix every section request caches does not change); every definition names an observable response the child makes (the dead-man test), and definitions that could score the same response carry exclusions that name each other (her questionnaire answers of 2026-11-27, p2q16). Written from her patterns, in our own words. | Only in the task of the case map's behaviors request. |
| `definition-review.md` | The AI review of the behaviors' definitions before QA (T3): the dead-man test and two definitions that would score the same response, reported in English by behavior name, never rewritten. | As the whole system text of the review request, after each map draft and when she asks for it again. Not part of the cached prefix. |
| `sections/medical-necessity.md` | What the Medical Necessity section is for, the structure the BCBA requires and what a good one looks like. | Only when generating that section, after the cached prefix. |
| `sections/*.md` (the other eight) | Each narrative section of the report: what it is for, what to write, its gaps, and what the decisions file does not settle yet ("Not yet settled with the BCBA"). Confirmed by her questionnaire answers of 2026-11-27, which also give what ABA Matrix or the agency fills in and is never drafted (the guide lists it). | Only when generating that section. |

Each file starts with an `id`, a `version`, the date it was `updated` and, where her answers cover it, `confirmed:`. A section's file may also give `paragraphs: 7-10` and `words: 900-1250`: the structure check beside the text uses them, and a section without them is checked for plain paragraphs only. When the content changes, the version goes up; every output stores the versions that produced it (`rulesVersions` in each run's JSON). The front matter ranges are the check's limits and allow a margin around the usual length the rules' prose gives ("about 300 to 400 words").

The order matters for cost: the system files (`assessment.md`, `guide.md`, `style.md`, `examples.md`, the payer facts and the instruments) are the same for every section and every case, and a case's sources are the same for every section of that case. That prefix is written once to the provider's cache and the other sections read it at a fraction of the price. Each section's rules go last so they don't break that prefix.
