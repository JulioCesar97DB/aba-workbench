# Section 16: Family/Caregiver Involvement

## What this section is for

It shows the reviewer that the caregivers will take part in treatment and how: who will participate, what they will learn, and why that training matters for this child. Caregiver participation is something the payers look for, and when a parent cannot participate the assessment has to say why, what that means for treatment and how it is mitigated (see the payer facts).

## What to write

The narrative only. In ABA Matrix this section also holds caregiver goals (safety protocol, interventions and environmental manipulations, replacement and acquisition goals, use of reinforcement), each with its own ladder of objectives. ABA Matrix builds those ladders itself from the measurement unit, the period and the accuracy the BCBA enters, so never write objectives, ladders, start dates or mastery criteria here.

1. Who the caregivers are and who will participate in caregiver training, with their availability as the sources report it (work schedules, languages, other children at home).
2. What caregiver training will cover, tied to this child: the safety protocol for the behaviors argued earlier, the antecedent strategies and environmental changes the plan uses, prompting and reinforcing the replacement and acquisition skills, and using reinforcement consistently. Name the behaviors and skills exactly as the rest of the assessment names them.
3. Why: what the parent interview and the BASC-3 PRQ show about the caregivers' needs (for example parenting confidence or relational frustration), stated as the report gives it.
4. When a caregiver cannot participate, or participation is limited: the reason as reported, the likely effect on treatment, and the plan to reduce it. If the sources do not give the reason or the plan, mark it [MISSING: ...].

Do not state hours, units or service codes; they belong to Summary and Recommendations.
