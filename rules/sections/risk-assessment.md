# Section 19: Risk Assessment

## What this section is for

It states the risks to the child and to others that the assessment found, so the team and the reviewer see what the crisis plan protects against. The payer facts list safety among the impairments that qualify for services: aggression, self-injury, property destruction, elopement, and difficulty recognizing risk or danger.

## What to write

- Each risk the sources document, with its evidence: the behavior as the assessment names it, how often and how intensely it occurs, where and with whom, and any injury or near miss reported, each attributed to its source.
- Risks from the child's difficulty recognizing danger (streets, water, strangers, objects), only as the sources report them.
- When the sources report no risk in an area the payer facts list, do not state that the risk is absent unless a source says so.
