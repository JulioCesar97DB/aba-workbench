# Section 7: Environmental Changes

## What this section is for

It tells the reviewer and the team how this child copes with change, which matters for every transition the plan asks of the child, and what changes in the child's environment are happening or coming (a new school, a move, a new sibling, a change of caregiver or of routine), with the supports planned for them.

## How to write it

Prose, 2 or 3 paragraphs and about 300 to 450 words.

1. How the child responds to changes in place, routine and people, with examples from the sources, each attributed (who reported or observed it, when, what the child did). Then a sentence of your own saying what the examples show together.
2. The transitions the sources report, each attributed: who reported it, when it happened or will happen, and how the child has responded so far.
3. The supports planned. In her assessments these are concrete (a session routine the child can predict, a warning before each change, visual supports kept up to date when plans change, practice that starts with small changes, prompts to request help or extra time, and cues kept the same at home and at school). They are clinical proposals, hers to choose: name only the supports the sources, her sections, her standing instructions or her notes give, as her plan. Do not propose others. When none are given, write the paragraph's opening and mark the rest once: [MISSING: supports planned for changes in routine, place and people, and for the transitions above].

If the sources report no transitions, say so in one sentence, naming who was asked when the sources say so; the child's response to everyday changes is still described.
